See the [workbook diff](catalog_patches/ll44-waste-soil-diff.md) and [controlled comparisons](LL_44_COMPARISONS.md). No default settings change.

The windrow, both carbon sources and the laundry are ordinary workbook flows: they run on real inputs and labor, with no new toggle. The windrow's mass loss (45 lb in, 25 lb out, plus water) is explicit. Laundry Station is tagged Sanitation and Tools rather than Water-System, which already has the 20 systems its category target allows.

## Carbon

The playtest's sanitation collapse came from composting toilets running out of Carbon, whose only sources were trees, a few plants and the market. Leaf litter trades about 78 hours a year for 1,564 lb; sawdust trades $313 and 13 hours for 5,214 lb. With either, a composting outhouse with no starting carbon goes from 0% to about 94–96% of sanitation met; the remaining gap is the outhouse's existing organic-waste timing, the same as with ample carbon.

## Cover crop seeding

This needs **Settings → Experimental mechanics → Soil fertility** (LL-35). The kit has no footprint and covers 200 sq ft of gardens within 30 ft, shared by garden area like LL-35's clover. Its seed is sown in a late-summer window (weeks 35–41, set by a flow timing override; the annual quantity keeps workbook parity). The share of seed actually received in that window is saved on the kit as `sown` and lasts until the next window.

On days outside the growing season, each covered garden gains 0.15 ÷ rest days × covered share × yesterday's upkeep met × sown share. The clover still works only in the growing season, so the two do not overlap. A kit with no seed, out of range, or with the gate off adds nothing. The card shows the gardens in range, the area covered and the seed sown.

## Measured

`docs/LL_44_COMPARISONS.md`, reproduced with `node --import tsx packages/cli/bots/waste-soil.ts`, three sites, Standard seed 1, full year, municipal water so water never limits:

- **Manure:** a 12-hen coop makes 926 lb of manure. A windrow takes 924 lb with 462 lb carbon and makes 770 lb of compost for 30.8 h. The coop supplies 60% of the windrow's input, so the windrow runs at that share.
- **Carbon:** as above.
- **Cover crop:** a bed at 0.5 fertility with no compost ends the year at 0.298; with a kit sown in late summer it ends at 0.354–0.359. Only November–December gain in the first year, because nothing was sown the autumn before.
- **Laundry:** 1,040 gal hot water → 2,340 gal greywater and 260 wellbeing points for 104 h a year.

Fixtures supply household food and cash and disable wellbeing drift. No survival-route gain is claimed.

## Quantities and evidence

All ratios, yields, prices, coverage and gains are experimental game balance, labeled in the workbook notes. Composting manure needs added carbon to balance its nitrogen, and composting loses mass as water and gas; the game makes both explicit rather than claiming a specific C:N recipe. No outside quantity is cited.

## Tests

Unit: windrow compost, mass loss and no run without carbon; leaf litter labor and sawdust cash; sawdust restoring a composting outhouse to its ample-carbon level; winter gain matching the formula with none in the growing season; sowing only received seed in the window, and an unsown kit adding nothing; gate, range and coverage share; laundry hot water, greywater and a Hot water limit; JSON continuation. Catalog: workbook rows, three Manure consumers, seeding rule validation. Browser: the seeding card and sawdust carbon.

## Limits

Leaf litter and sawdust arrive evenly through the year, not at leaf fall. The windrow has no pile temperature, curing time or pathogen kill. The seeding kit has no species, termination date or nitrogen accounting. Laundry soap and drying are abstracted; human water use is unchanged, so laundry is an extra, explicit activity rather than a split of it. LL-45 is next.
