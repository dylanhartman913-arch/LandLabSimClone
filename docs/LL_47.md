# LL-47: catalog audit gates

Implemented October 25, 2026. No simulation change, so the engine stays at **0.25.0**; no workbook rows.

The audit (`catalogAudit` in `packages/engine/src/views/audit.ts`) checks the exported catalog against the three sites, and `packages/engine/test/catalog-audit.test.ts` enforces three gates:

1. **No dead ends:** a Flow resource that something makes must have a use: a system input, a checklist need it feeds or substitutes for, an engine rule that consumes it (preservation recipes, manure on gardens, hatching eggs), or a market price. Wool, the roadmap's example, now has two uses: LL-29's raw sale and LL-46's spinning wheel.
2. **Two non-market sources for survival inputs:** every Flow input of a home system that makes water, drinking water, food, shelter, sanitation or heat needs at least two home producers or kinds of natural node. Conventional systems and backstops do not count.
3. **No unsuppliable systems:** every system needs at least one site where each input has a source: a producer that can run there (a producer that needs a missing stream cannot), a natural node, the site's ambient inputs or a market listing. This is one level deep: a source must exist, not prove its own inputs.

Results are published between markers in `docs/CATALOG.md` by `npm run catalog:audit`, which exits 1 on a failure. The test fails if that section is out of date.

## Results and exceptions

No dead ends. No unsuppliable system: 204 of 205 systems can be supplied at all three sites; the micro-hydro turbine needs Asheville's stream.

Nine survival inputs have fewer than two non-market sources. Each is a reviewed exception in `data/catalog_overrides.json` (`auditExceptions`), with a reason and, where one makes sense, a follow-up:

| Input | Why it is accepted | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Chicken feed | One home mix plus the store; birds also draw greens, grubs and scraps directly | Forage or scrap ration |
| Gasoline | Fossil fuel; reaches survival only through the camper van shelter | — |
| Grain | The grain plot keeps its own seed; no seed-grain market | Seed-grain listing or a second grain crop |
| Milk | Only the optional cheese press uses it | — |
| Root crops | The potato patch keeps its own seed potatoes; no seed-potato market | Seed-potato listing |
| Seedlings | Seed starting station plus the store | Cuttings or divisions |
| Waste lumber | Market only; feeds heat only through the biochar retort | Salvage or sawmill offcuts |
| Wood chips | Chipper only; feeds the retort and pellet mill, neither essential for heat | Tree-service chip drops |
| Wood pellets | Pellet mill plus the store; firewood heats every shelter | — |

The test fails on any new dead end, fragile survival input or unsuppliable system, and also on a stale exception whose finding is gone. The list can only shrink. The grain and root-crop gaps are real: a first plot needs seed in stock, because no market sells it.

## Tests

Six: the shipped catalog passes with no stale exception; the published section matches; fixtures prove each gate fires (Coffee grounds with its users removed is a dead end; removing the exceptions fails exactly the nine fragile inputs; a stale exception fails; removing the chipper's output makes the retort and pellet mill unsuppliable; micro-hydro is suppliable only at Asheville).

## Limits

Supply is one level deep; survival makers are found from workbook output flows and needs rows, not from simulated survival. Source counts treat each producer and each kind of node as one source, regardless of size or season.
