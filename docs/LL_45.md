# LL-45: heat, hot water and shelter efficiency

Implemented October 25, 2026 in engine **0.24.0**. Three workbook-backed systems added with `scripts/catalog_patch.py`; existing typed workbook values remain unchanged. See the [workbook diff](catalog_patches/ll45-heat-efficiency-diff.md) and [controlled comparisons](LL_45_COMPARISONS.md). No default settings change and no toggle: each works when placed.

| System | Catalog row | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas Tent Winter Liner | $180, 0.05 h/week, no footprint | Placed on a canvas tent, yurt or teepee: its heat need × 0.75 |
| Wood Boiler with Water Jacket | 50 lb wood + 0.5 h/week → 150,000 BTU Heat + 140 gal Hot water | Fired year-round: hot water every week, heat in the heating season |
| Draft-Proofing Kit (small shelter) | $120, 0.02 h/week, no footprint | Placed on a camper, camper van, solar cabin, tiny house, container, earthbag dome or treehouse: heat need × 0.85, cooling need × 0.9 |

## Shelter efficiency

The liner and kit are host modifiers, the mechanism the weatherization retrofit and wool bedding already use: they act on the shelter they sit on and on nothing else. The liner matches the Canvas category; a shade sail is Canvas but has no heat need, so it is unaffected. Draft-proofing lists its seven shelters (all 400 sq ft or less, none canvas). Modifiers multiply: liner plus bedding gives 0.75 × 0.2. Draft-proofing also multiplies with the whole-house retrofit, which overstates the saving if a player buys both; that is noted in the row.

## Boiler and hot water

The boiler's wood input is steady by a flow-timing override (heating-only systems otherwise burn on the heating curve), because it is fired for hot water all year. Its heat output still follows the heating season, so summer heat is made and unused. Its 140 gal/week covers one adult's 40 gal with room for a laundry station (LL-44).

**Decision: Hot water stays out of the scored checklist.** The workbook marks it "not a checklist row", it already drifts wellbeing as a comfort need (weight 0.15), and a scored row would change every design's coverage score and break spreadsheet parity. Instead the measured checklist now shows an unscored line under the table: "Hot water (comfort, not scored): X% of Y gal a week met", with its own explanation. The report's "short" now has a visible place in the checklist.

## Measured

`docs/LL_45_COMPARISONS.md`, reproduced with `node --import tsx packages/cli/bots/heat-efficiency.ts`, Standard seed 1:

- **Liner on the starter tent** (tiny stove and bedding, December–February): heat need falls by 25% at every site (Front Range 4.18 → 3.13 MBTU). In Laramie, heat met rises from 80.5% to 98.9%. The stove burns its catalog wood either way, because it also cooks.
- **Draft-proofed tiny house:** heat need falls 15% (Laramie 13.75 → 11.69 MBTU/yr) and cooling need 10%.
- **Hot water for one adult:** the boiler, the propane tankless heater and the solar heater all meet 99.7% (the first day lands at day's end). The boiler burns 2,607 lb of wood a year; the propane heater 31.3 gal.

Fixtures supply food, water, firewood and cash and disable wellbeing drift. No survival-route gain is claimed.

## Quantities and evidence

The 25%, 15% and 10% reductions, the boiler's output split and all costs are experimental game assumptions, labeled in the workbook notes. No outside quantity is cited.

## Tests

Unit: the liner's 25% on a canvas tent, nothing beside it or on a hard shelter, stacking with bedding; draft-proofing's heat and cooling on a tiny house and nothing on a tent; the boiler's steady wood and hot water in winter and summer with heat mostly in winter; no output without wood; the checklist's unscored hot water line at 100% and 0% with no Hot water row or score reference; JSON continuation. Catalog: workbook rows, modifier targets (small, non-canvas shelters) and boiler timings. Browser: a liner on the camp's bell tent and the checklist comfort line.

## Limits

Fixed multipliers: no condensation, ventilation, R-values or liner wear beyond LL-28. The boiler has no tank losses or summer heat dumping. Hot water remains a comfort need outside the coverage score. LL-46 (income and trade) is next.
