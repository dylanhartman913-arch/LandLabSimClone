# LL-37: finance

Implemented October 25, 2026 in engine **0.17.0**. Enable **Settings → Experimental mechanics → Credit, tax and insurance**, then open **Market → Finance**. New games and continuation migrations leave this gate off. [Measured comparisons](LL_37_COMPARISONS.md) reproduce 36 matched first-year runs plus three controlled failure cases.

## Credit and tax

The household can explicitly draw on a **$5,000 credit line at 12% annual interest**, or repay any positive amount covered by cash and debt. Borrowing never happens automatically. Purchases, delivery fees, connection fees and running costs obey the cash floor whenever Finance is enabled, even in saves that have not enabled `cashFloor`.

Interest is assessed daily on outstanding debt and paid from cash after the day's operating expenses and income. Unpaid interest becomes labeled debt; it can exceed the draw limit, but never increases available credit. There is no minimum principal repayment. On opting in, an existing legacy cash deficit is converted to opening debt, with a corresponding financing entry, rather than left as negative cash.

Property tax is assessed daily at **1% of $10,000 per parcel acre per year**. Available cash pays the accrued bill after interest. Anything unpaid remains in **Unpaid property tax**, and later cash settles it. No penalty interest, tax seizure or amortizing mortgage is modeled.

## Optional equipment failure insurance

Insurance costs **$180 per year**, assessed daily after interest and tax. Coverage applies only when that day's premium can be paid in full; an unfunded day has no coverage or partial premium charge. The choice stays enabled, so coverage resumes on a funded day.

With LL-28 wear enabled, an active physical asset crossing from positive condition to zero pays **80% of its scaled catalog replacement value above a $250 per-asset deductible**. It pays once at failure, without requiring a repair purchase. An already-failed asset cannot claim, nor can a removed asset, an asset under repair, routine maintenance or livestock. There is no weather/fire liability policy; LL-38 risk events remain a separate ticket. This deliberately narrow policy gives the optional insurance a working covered loss now.

## Reserve and accounting

The configurable **1–365 day reserve target** defaults to 90 days. It multiplies positive daily net operating spending over up to 28 measured days by the chosen days. Before seven measured days, it uses scheduled interest, tax and premium costs. With non-positive measured net spending, the target is zero; this is a trailing cash-runway measure, not a seasonal forecast. The shortfall adds unpaid tax to the target, then subtracts available cash. Available credit is shown separately and never counts as a reserve.

Borrowing, principal repayment and insurance payouts do not distort the cash runway or earn the net-positive-season milestone. Daily cash still reconciles exactly, including actions between days. Weekly bills identify paid interest, property tax and premiums; the Finance panel shows recent payouts and capitalized interest. Reports label borrowing, repayments and claims within their cash totals. Unpaid tax and capitalized interest are liabilities, not cash bills already paid.

All rates are **experimental game balance**, stored in `data/catalog_overrides.json`, checked by the catalog schema and exported into the generated catalog. They are not sourced market quotes or location-specific tax estimates. No workbook rows or dependencies were added.

## Controlled results

Across Front Range, Laramie and Asheville (Standard seed 1; Real seeds 1–3), the matched remote-job route has no survival-short days with any variant. Its one-acre parcel pays $100 tax over the year. Keeping a $2,000 draw outstanding adds $240 interest; insurance adds $180 premiums. Cash less outstanding debt therefore ends $100 below legacy for finance alone, and $520 below legacy for the insured loan. No asset fails on the ordinary first-year route, so it receives no claims. The controlled Bell Tent failure pays $440 with a paid premium, zero without insurance or cash, and never pays again the next day.

## Verification and save behavior

Engine regressions cover credit limits, invalid amounts, repayment affordability, daily/annual costs, conservation, arrears, interest beyond the limit, purchase blocking without `cashFloor`, runway/reserve provenance, insured/uninsured/unfunded failures, no repeat or repair claims, reports, milestones, legacy deficit conversion, opt-out, action replay and JSON continuation. Catalog validation rejects invalid limits, rates, premiums, coverage and reserve days. A browser test enables the gate, borrows, rejects an over-limit draw, repays, buys insurance, sets the reserve and checks measured bills.

Turning Finance off pauses charges and preserves debt, tax due and policy preferences, matching the other experimental mechanics. Re-enabling resumes from those saved liabilities. Pending draws/repayments remain accounted for even if the gate is turned off before the next day. No automatic experimental migration is introduced.
