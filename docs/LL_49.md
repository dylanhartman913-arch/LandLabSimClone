# LL-49: alert-coverage tests

Implemented October 25, 2026. No simulation or alert change, so the engine stays at **0.25.0**.

`packages/cli/bots/coverage.ts` plays the LL-48 novice bot and checks, for six survival cliffs, that one of the cliff's alerts was on the Needs and bottlenecks list every morning for at least a week before the cliff bit. `packages/cli/test/coverage.test.ts` enforces it in CI (the unit job), and `npm run sim -- coverage --md docs/LL_49_COVERAGE.md` regenerates the published table, exiting 1 on a miss.

## What counts

| Cliff | Cliff day | Alerts that count | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food | first Food wellbeing penalty | `food-runway`, `food` (Act now or Coming up) | 7 |
| Safe water | first Drinking water penalty | `water-runway`, `drinkingWater` | 7 |
| Carbon | first Sanitation penalty after Carbon runs short | `Carbon`, `sanitation` | 7 |
| Fuel | first Heat penalty after firewood or propane runs short | `Woody biomass`, `Propane`, `heat` | 7 |
| Chicken feed | first day feed runs short (feed has no wellbeing penalty) | `Chicken feed` (also Supplies for production) | 6 |
| Cash | first day cash ends below $1 (cash has no penalty of its own) | `cash-runway` | 7 |

- A penalty is a wellbeing term past its grace days ("Food: 30% met for 6 days"), read from each resident's terms the next morning.
- Lead is the number of mornings in a row, ending on the cliff day, that a counting alert was listed in an allowed section. An alert that appeared once in spring and went away does not count for a cliff in autumn.
- The 90-day forecast is reported but never counted: it names the starting stockpile's end on day 1 of nearly every run, which would make every food lead huge.
- Feed's six days are the consumable alert's design: it lists once fewer than seven whole days remain, which is six full days plus the partial one.

## Scenarios

Real weather, 365 days, the rules a new game sets, on every site (six since LL-51):

1. **Novice gate:** the LL-48 novice on the seasonal line, acting on Act now, seeds 1–3. It reaches no cliff at all.
2. **October 22 playtest:** the original quest line, alerts ignored, seeds 1–3. Carbon on every site (sanitation penalty day 90, warned 10–11 days ahead, at the top of the list); firewood in Laramie (heat penalty day 120, warned 17–19 days ahead).
3. **No automatic water gathering** (seed 1): safe water, warned exactly 7 days ahead on every site.
4. **$600 starting cash** (seed 1): cash runs out on day 160 (warned 19–29 days ahead), feed a week later (6 days), then food (7–9 days); Laramie also runs out of firewood (8 days).

The test also requires each cliff to be reached on every site, so it can't pass by never testing one, with one exception: only Laramie's novice runs out of fuel before a heat penalty, because elsewhere the cold quest's wood order arrives in time. Two fixtures prove the check has teeth: hiding the water runway alert leaves only the shortage itself and fails, and an alert moved to Comfort counts for nothing.

Full table: `docs/LL_49_COVERAGE.md`.

## Changes

- `noviceBot` records every alert it sees each morning (`shown`: id, section, rank, resource). Its behavior is unchanged.
- `playBot` accepts a start definition as well as a start id, for stress starts.

## Limits

Seven days is the Act now horizon, not a measured reaction time. The stress starts are edits to the greenfield kit, not new starts. Cliffs are measured for the greenfield start only (the adapt start has backstops for every survival need). Water and food leads sit right at the threshold: any later runway warning fails the test.
