# MP-04 — Learn and collaborate

Status: implementation built; automated checks complete; uncoached human and physical-device acceptance pending.

## Delivered

- The lobby's **How to play together** and in-game **Invite & room rules** share the same instructions: one budget and inventory, simulated residents and labor, resource dependencies, informal responsibilities, time controls, shortages and recovery, persistent saves and per-tab seat recovery limits.
- A first-session guide suggests a collaborative opening water milestone within a 10–15 minute session, without promising full tutorial completion in that time.
- An always-visible shared objective shows completed quests out of 11, the next quest and its measured progress, and an explicit completion message with continued sandbox play.
- Quest measurements are calculated from the server's retained history before network history is trimmed. Both players receive the same quest display; browser-local tutorial dismissal does not hide the shared tutorial. The unsupported shared Skip button is hidden.
- Multiplayer-only layout changes keep room instructions and long quest cards scrollable, wrap panel navigation, and provide larger tablet time controls and drawer toggle. Single-player styling remains unchanged.
- No dependencies, new mechanics or infrastructure added.

At the fixed room speed, 213 running days alone take 3 minutes 33 seconds. Decisions, construction/harvest/alert pauses and two-player readiness add real time. A bot run does not measure novice learning time or include multiplayer pause handling. Validate the first-session guide with people; do not advertise full completion in 15 minutes.

## Owner acceptance checks

1. **Two uncoached players, 15–20 minutes:** open the public multiplayer entry, create and invite without owner credentials, read only the on-page guidance. Each person should explain the shared budget, why a human joining adds no simulated labor, and why responsibilities reserve nothing. Each makes a useful edit. Can they reach the first water milestone and find the next instruction without help? Record confusing labels or steps.
2. **Two physical laptops:** each independently opens Market, Work and the catalog, builds something, reads shared quest progress, and uses Ready/Pause. Confirm both see matching cash, day and milestones while camera and selection remain independent.
3. **Real tablet:** tap controls, scroll quest/rules panels, open/close the catalog, buy and place a system, pan empty land and pinch zoom. Try portrait and landscape. Note accidental placement, blocked taps or text that is too small. Browser touch emulation does not establish physical touch usability. Phone support is not claimed.
