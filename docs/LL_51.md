# LL-51: more sites and starts

Implemented October 25, 2026 in engine **0.26.0**. Three sites, two starts and children in the household. Existing sites, starts and saves are unchanged: the LL-50 comparisons (original three sites) match their committed headlines exactly.

## Sites

Each is a `data/sites/*.json` preset with approximate NOAA 1991–2020 normals (HDD/CDD base 65°F) and NREL solar resource. Monthly shapes add up to the annual totals within 3% (tested).

| Site | Climate | What makes it hard | Land |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sonoran desert, Arizona** (`tucson-az`) | 1,500 HDD, 3,600 CDD (the most of any site), 6.4 sun hours, 11.2 in of rain, mostly in the July–September monsoon; two growing seasons (day 59–330) | Water and cooling | No creek. A seep in the wash yields 10 gal/h of work (40% of a mountain spring) and less in a dry year; two rain pools fill in the monsoon and dry fast; slow mesquite deadfall |
| **Puget Sound lowland, Washington** (`olympia-wa`) | 5,500 HDD, 120 CDD, 3.4 sun hours (the least), 49 in of rain (the most), October–April | Mold and blight, low winter sun | Abundant water: a year-round creek (stream flow, so micro-hydro works) and a spring that does not fail in a dry year; big conifer deadfall, blackberries, leaf litter |
| **Lake Superior shore, Michigan** (`marquette-mi`) | 8,600 HDD, 180 CDD, 3.8 sun hours, 33 in of precipitation (about 150 in of snow); growing season day 145–273 | Lake-effect snow, short season | Hardwood deadfall, a spring, blueberries, leaf litter, rain pools |

**Hazards.** Sites can carry an optional `hazards` block: `riskChanceMult` scales an LL-38 risk kind's chance per 28-day interval (capped at 1), only when **Warned risk events** is on. Tucson: wildfire smoke ×2, well drawdown ×1.5. Olympia: garden disease ×2 (mold and blight). Marquette: blizzards ×2 (roads closed by lake-effect snow). The two random draws per kind per interval happen either way, so a multiplier never moves any other draw (tested). Sites without `hazards`, which is all three originals, behave exactly as before.

**The Tucson seep.** The first draft had no reliable water at all, and the idle and tutorial households died of thirst by day 40 because the rain pools are empty in a dry spring. A 6 gal/h seep was still too little for the tutorial follower once the garden, hens and quail drew water; 10 gal/h passes every gate. The tutorial's cold-snap quest never comes in the desert, so the tutorial follower finishes six of seven quests there.

## Mortgage (LL-37 finance)

`FinanceState.mortgage` holds `{ balance, annualRate, payment, arrears }`. Each day, after credit interest and before property tax: the balance accrues a day's interest, then the day's installment (12 monthly payments ÷ 365) plus any arrears is paid from cash. Whatever cash can't cover waits in arrears, paid first on a later day; there is no penalty interest or foreclosure. Payments are in `cash.out` and the cash runway, so the cash alert warns ahead of them. Turning Finance off pauses the mortgage, like the other finance charges. The Finance panel shows the balance, rate, monthly payment and arrears, each with its Why.

A year with ample cash pays exactly 12 monthly payments; the $280,000 balance falls by about $3,100 of principal in year 1.

## Households

The start screen offers children (0 up to the start's `maxChildren`) next to the 1–4 adults. A child is a Human Being with the LL-36 `child` role (70% of an adult's needs, 25% of the work), so choosing children turns on **Household roles, skills and health**. The stockpile scales with what the household eats (a child counts as 0.7). `GameInit.start.children` replays the choice.

**Kits grow with the household** (follow-up, October 25). Each start lists `household.extra` blocks: one copy for every `per` people beyond `after` (default: the default household), counted by need (a child as 0.7) or by adults. Copy k sits `offset` × k from the listed spot, or at the nearest free spot. The default household gets no blocks, so default games are byte-identical (tested on all four starts). The start screen lists what a household adds (`householdKit`).

| Start | Per 2 more people | Other blocks |
|---|---|---|
| adapt | a car, two compost toilets, 208 lbs of sawdust | a job per 2 more adults |
| greenfield | another camp: tent, bedding, tiny stove, fan, 500 W panel, power station, filter, rain barrel, bucket toilet, 100 lbs of sawdust | — |
| cabin | composting outhouse, rocket stove, 500 W panel, filter, 150 lbs of sawdust | a bell tent with its own stove and bedding per 1.5 people past 3 |
| farm | septic field, water filter | a job per 2 more adults; a tiny house per 2 people past 4 |

`npm run sim -- households` (published in `docs/HOUSEHOLDS.md`) checks every start, site and household the start screen allows, 144 cases: no kit row (drinking water, shelter, heat on a cold day, sanitation) may start more than 5 points below the default household's on day 3, and the idle household must stay home within 21 days of the default one. The 21 days allow for wild food, which doesn't grow with the household. Before scaling 48 of 144 passed; now all 144 do. Examples: greenfield with 4 adults left on day 17 and now leaves with the default household (day 71–72, when the idle stockpile runs out); farm and adapt families of up to 7 stay home all year. The novice who acts on alerts now keeps every greenfield household home all year (it left on day 14–17 for 2+2 and up).

The cabin's $6,000 savings don't grow with the household, so a bigger cabin household that buys its food runs out of money sooner: the novice leaves around day 183–197 with 2 adults + 2 children and day 135–138 with 4 + 3, against day 261–278 for two adults.

| Gate | Bot | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `cabin-idle` | idle, Standard | no hardship for 28 days; nobody leaves before day 60 (first hardship day 46–47) |
| `cabin-novice` | novice, Real | stays home through day 240; every departure warned 14+ days ahead (leaves day 261–278 when the cash runs out) |
| `farm-idle` | idle, Standard | no installment missed, nobody leaves, wellbeing ≥ 70 all year ($23,500–$24,200 at the end) |
| `farm-family` | idle, Real, 2 adults + 2 children | home all year with no installment missed |

All 10 gates pass on all 6 sites and 3 seeds. A farm without its job fails `farm-idle` (tested). LL-49's alert coverage now also runs on all six sites and passes; fuel is still reached only in Laramie.

## Limits

- Roof snow load, indoor mold and desert heat stress on people are not modeled; the hazards only change how often existing LL-38 events come.
- Site normals are approximate and rounded; real weather varies them the same way as the original sites.
- No foreclosure, refinancing, extra principal payments or variable rates. Arrears carry no penalty.
- Children never grow up. The cabin's savings and the greenfield tutorial's rewards don't grow with the household.
- The new starts have no quest line, and the LL-10 comparisons still cover only the greenfield start on the original three sites.
