# LL-38: warned risk events

Implemented October 25, 2026 in engine **0.18.0**. Enable **Settings → Experimental mechanics → Warned risk events**. Startup and continuation leave it off. Warnings appear in **Needs and bottlenecks** with deadlines and mitigation instructions; events reach toasts and the Almanac's Weather filter. With hardship auto-pause enabled, a new warning stops the clock. [Measured comparisons](LL_38_COMPARISONS.md) reproduce 48 matched first-year runs and controlled mitigation scenarios.

## Events and mitigation

Every 28 days, each new hazard takes two draws from its own saved random stream, regardless of eligibility or defenses. An eligible successful draw schedules a warning; nothing happens immediately. The start is the warning day plus the configured lead **plus one**, giving the player the full number of intervening days. At most one threat of each kind is outstanding. Garden and equipment risks select one eligible asset; infrastructure/weather risks affect all matching sources.

| Risk | Eligibility and chance per interval | Full warning days | Effect and duration | Mitigation |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Equipment breakdown | LL-28 enabled; an active physical asset with positive condition below 60%; 25% | 5 | Condition falls to zero at the deadline; it remains failed until serviced | Repair or replace the warned asset. Work in progress at the deadline also averts that warning. Normal wear and its lower output continue separately. |
| Garden pests | A modeled garden during the growing season; 25% | 5 | Target garden harvest/by-products × 0.5 for 7 days | An operating Blue Birds, Bat House or Native Hedgerow within 100 ft softens the multiplier to 0.85. |
| Garden disease | A modeled garden during the growing season; 18% | 5 | Target garden harvest/by-products × 0.5 for 10 days | Operating Compost Piles within 100 ft soften the multiplier to 0.85 as an experimental soil-care rule. |
| Wildfire smoke | June–August; 25% | 3 | +8 cooling degree days, plus up to 1.5 wellbeing points/day of air exposure for 5 days | Operating enclosed houses/cabins reduce exposure by 75% for the share of residents their actual catalog living space covers. Keep cooling supplied. Tents, closed homes and failed assets provide no refuge. |
| Blizzard | At least 30 heating degree days on the draw day; 30% | 3 | Market trips and delivery backstops stop for 3 days | Stock food, water, feed and fuel in storage before roads close. Orders wait and resume once; there are no delivery charges or repeated standing top-ups during closure. Piped water and utility power remain separate. |
| Grid outage | Active grid connection; 18% | 3 | Grid electricity stops for 2 days | Charged batteries, independent generation and a fueled backup generator can serve loads. Fixed connection fees continue, and other utility outputs remain governed by their own rules. |
| Well drawdown | A well, growing season and Real-weather precipitation multiplier below 0.8; 50% | 7 | Both powered and hand-pumped well water output × 0.35 for 14 days | Fill a cistern beforehand; add rainwater collection or another non-well source. Stored water and rain collection are unaffected. |
| Predators | Existing LL-31 checks when livestock lifecycle is enabled | Existing 3-day warning | Existing warned attack, animal loss or protection | A fed, staffed guardian in range or operating secure housing. LL-38 surfaces this warning without adding another predator draw or attack. |

Modeled gardens use the existing soil-fertility garden list, without requiring the fertility gate. Pest and disease multipliers stack if both affect the same garden; wellbeing and capacities are excluded. Effects end at the saved end day and their start/end is logged. The equipment event ending does **not** restore condition.

Defenses must be completed and full scale, at least 80% operational on the **previous measured day**, and currently usable. This prevents an unfinished, unfunded or already-broken defense from granting protection by placement alone. The one-day readiness delay is explicit in warnings. Enclosed refuge capacity is shared among the household, or among residents assigned to that home under LL-32; overflow and residents in tents still receive smoke exposure. Air exposure is a named wellbeing term and follows difficulty scaling and the normal daily loss cap; it is not a new catalog resource or a medical simulation.

## Accounting, saves and gates

Market closure returns before orders are drained or standing orders expanded. Backstop delivery capacity and grid electricity obey the same risk multipliers shown in source/card provenance. Independent sources, stored stocks, and the normal cash/input limits still apply. The new hazards never grant free supplies.

A newly failed LL-28 asset qualifies for the existing LL-37 equipment insurance if that day's premium can be paid; the failure is visible to Finance before the cash ledger is finalized. One failure cannot pay twice. Ordinary repair costs and labor still apply.

The optional `risks` state holds pending/active threats and measured defense readiness; `riskRng` is separate from weather, livestock and household streams. Both participate in replay digests and JSON continuation. Daily ledgers list active hazards, and events preserve warnings, starts, ends and averted breakdowns. Disabling the gate clears pending events and readiness, stops their effects, and freezes the saved risk stream; it does not restore equipment condition or reverse prior losses. Re-enabling starts new future draws. Legacy games never acquire these fields or any new risk effects. See [SAVES.md](SAVES.md).

All chances, durations, loss factors, mitigation/source lists and damage costs come from validated engine overrides and the existing catalog. They are **experimental game balance**, not empirical weather forecasts, pest/disease models or location-specific risk estimates. No new workbook systems or dependencies were added. LL-28 and LL-31 remain separate opt-ins.

## Controlled results

Across Front Range, Laramie and Asheville, Standard seed 1 and Real seeds 1–3, the greenfield remote-job route retains zero survival-short days. Risk exposure lowers its end cash by $0–$1,238, through the normal household/work/purchase loops. The adapt-upgrader route has 0–12 survival-short days with risk on versus zero with it off, principally exposing its grid dependency; its end cash changes by −$247 to +$15 (lower bills during outages are not evidence of better service). Minimum wellbeing across these first-year routes remains 75.2 greenfield and 75.8 adapt; the minimum occurs outside the later transient exposure.

Controlled scenarios show harvest rising from 0.059 to 0.100 lb/day with a working nearby defense; a timely repair averting failure rather than collecting a $440 insurance payout; enclosed refuge lowering air exposure from 1.5 to 0.375 points/day; stored food supplying 6,000 kcal through a three-day road closure versus zero without reserves; independent fueled generation producing while grid imports are zero; and drawdown lowering the wells/rain mix from 196.15 to 89.36 gal/day. These comparisons do not change defaults or tune the first-year routes.

## Verification and limits

Regressions cover eligibility, complete warning periods, fixed draw counts, unchanged two-year weather draws and predator behavior, deadline failure and insurance, preemptive service, both garden losses and actual defense warm-up, ineffective distant/unfunded/partial/unfinished defenses, per-home smoke capacity and wellbeing, delivery preservation/resumption, independent power during outages, well/rain output, expiry, opt-out, replay and JSON continuation. Catalog checks reject missing lead times, invalid factors/chances and unknown mitigation systems. The browser flow reads a blizzard warning, queues an order, waits through closure, receives it once, and checks the Almanac reopening entry.

Limits: no spatial smoke plume, wildfire damage, pest species, disease transmission, aquifer volume or cumulative groundwater depletion, grid repair crews, snow-clearing action or hazard-responsive bot policy. Only configured delivered backstops stop in blizzards; town employment and trade trips are not a new transportation model (LL-39). Insurance still covers failed equipment only, not crop loss or outage income. The risk queue is interval-based, not a calibrated regional probability model.
